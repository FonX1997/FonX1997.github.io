About me关于我

I am an Assistant Research Fellow and Postdoctoral Researcher at Beihang University Hangzhou Innovation Institute, working with Academician Jiancheng Fang and Prof. Jianli Li. I received my Ph.D. in Electronic Information from Beihang University in 2025, supervised by Prof. Zhuo Wang.

My research connects artificial intelligence and intelligent control with atomic spin inertial sensing and quantum precision measurement. I develop physics-informed learning, reinforcement learning, adaptive dynamic programming, and digital-twin parallel control for system modeling, state estimation, and multiphysics disturbance compensation.

我现任北京航空航天大学杭州创新研究院助理研究员（博士后），合作导师为房建成院士、李建利教授。2025 年获北京航空航天大学电子信息博士学位，博士生导师为王卓教授。

我的研究围绕人工智能与智能控制算法及其在原子自旋惯性测量、量子精密测量中的应用展开，重点研究物理约束深度学习、强化学习、自适应动态规划与数字孪生平行控制，开展系统建模、状态估计及多物理场耦合扰动补偿。
